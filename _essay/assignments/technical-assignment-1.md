I lived (on and off) for more than two decades in Boudha, a neighborhood in east Kathmandu, and moved to Virginia in the summer of 2025. Most of the built environment of Boudha has been developed in the past fifty years, but the UNESCO monument in the center of the area, the Boudhanath Stupa, is at least 900 years old (and dates to the 6th century by legend). A stupa (also known as a chaitya) is a Buddhist reliquary monument which houses precious and sacred objects, built for the purpose of public worship and merit making. Wishing to have model of the stupa at home to remind me of my home away from home, I decided to take advantage of the 3D printers at UVA's Makerspace in the Scholars Lab at Shannon Library.


![Top view of the Boudha Stupa on terraprint.com](https://kilgoresmith.github.io/assets/img/terraprint_boudha_top.png)

To get the image, I searched for Kathmandu terraprinter.com, located Boudha and cut out the stupa. I downloaded the file as an .stl file.

![Cutting out the Boudha Stupa from terraprint.com](https://kilgoresmith.github.io/assets/img/terraprint_boudha_capture.png)
